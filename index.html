Welcome to the Dream

A little dream at the edge of the Arabian Sea

Dream Camp sits on the white-sand shore of Bar Al Hikman, a quiet peninsula on Oman’s Al Wusta coast where lagoons glow at sunrise and flamingos wade in the shallows.

Hand-painted signs, whale-bone arches, hammocks swaying in the sea breeze, and A-frame bungalows just steps from the water. We keep coastal glamping simple: great service, meals included, and memorable days between the sea and the stars.

  • 2–4guests per bungalow

A day at Dream Camp

Sunrise

Open the door to the sea.

Morning light floods your A-frame and the lagoon turns to glass. Step out barefoot onto the sand: breakfast is waiting.

Daytime

Turquoise water, all to yourself.

Paddle a kayak, join a volleyball match, or head out to the coral spot to snorkel, then glide past storks and flamingos on the lagoon.

Sunset

Golden hour in a hammock.

The sky melts into peach and gold. Swing in a hammock under the palm-frond shade and watch the sun slip into the sea.

Starlight

Bonfires and a sky full of stars.

Gather round the evening bonfire (it’s on us), then climb up to your terrace. Far from city lights, the stars put on the show.

    Good to know

    Questions, answered

    How do I book?

    Tap any “Book on WhatsApp” button and send us your dates and number of guests. We’ll confirm availability and share everything you need for your stay.

    What’s included in the price?

    A one-night stay includes dinner and breakfast. Book two nights or more and lunch on the day after check-in is included too.

    How many people fit in a bungalow?

    Each bungalow hosts 2 to 4 guests. Coming as a bigger group? Book several bungalows side by side.

    How much do children pay?

    Children aged 3 to 15 are OMR 30 per night. Travelling with a little one under 3? Just mention it in your message.

    Which activities cost extra?

    The snorkeling trip to the coral spot is OMR 10 per person and includes the lagoon tour with storks and flamingos. The fishing & crab-hunting trip is OMR 15 per person. The evening bonfire is free, and kayaks, volleyball and table games are available at camp.
